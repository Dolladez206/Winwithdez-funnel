## The ONLY thing a human has to do: post it
Everything is written. The single human step is **publishing** — that has to be
Desiree (it's her face + her accounts). Here's the lightest possible routine:

### Option A — 15 minutes a day (simplest)
1. Open **30-day-content-calendar.md**, find today's day number.
2. If it's a **video**: read the script into her phone camera (one take, be real,
   don't over-produce). If it's an **image/text post**: use the caption as-is.
3. Copy the **caption + hashtags**, post to Facebook (primary), then reshare the
   same thing to Instagram Reels and TikTok.
4. Done. Move to tomorrow's number tomorrow.

### Option B — Batch once a week (even easier, free)
- Facebook + Instagram let you **schedule posts for free** in **Meta Business
  Suite** (business.facebook.com → "Planner" → "Create post" → set date/time).
- Desiree films 5–7 videos in one sitting, then schedules the whole week.
  Now it posts itself, hands-off, all week.
- TikTok: its built-in scheduler (on desktop) also posts for free up to 10 days out.

**That's it.** Post daily (or batch weekly). The kit does everything else.

## The rules that protect her money (built into every post)
- ✅ Everything is branded **"Win With Dez"** and points to **winwithdez.com**.
- 🚫 We **never name the company** in public content — so nobody can google it
  and sign up under someone else's link. Her link stays the only door.
- 🚫 **No income promises. No health claims.** Every post is honest — which is
  exactly what makes skeptical people trust her.
