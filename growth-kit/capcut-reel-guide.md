# Make a Win With Dez Reel in CapCut (free, ~10 min)

CapCut is a free phone app. This turns any of Desiree's lifestyle clips into a
branded reel. Same steps every time.

## 1. Get the app + the clip
- Install **CapCut** (App Store / Google Play) — free.
- Make sure the video is saved to the **phone's camera roll** (from Google Drive:
  open the video → ⋮ or Share → **Download / Save to device**).

## 2. Start the project
- Open CapCut → **New Project** → select her clip → **Add**.

## 3. Trim to the best ~15 seconds
- Tap the clip on the timeline → drag the white handles at each end to keep only
  the best, liveliest part (about 15 seconds).

## 4. Add the 3 text overlays
Tap **Text → Add text**, type the line, then drag it where you want it on screen
and drag its bar on the timeline to set when it shows. Do this 3 times:
- **Living my best life ✨** — over the first few seconds
- **Freedom looks good on me** — middle
- **winwithdez.com** — hold this one to the very end
Style tip: pick a clean **bold white** font; under "Style" turn on a light
**shadow** or **background** so it's easy to read.

## 5. (Optional) green end card
- Tap **+** at the end of the timeline → add any short green image/clip (2 sec),
  or just let the **winwithdez.com** text sit over her last few seconds. Either works.

## 6. Keep her sound
- Do nothing — her singing/music is already in the clip. (Don't mute it.)

## 7. Export
- Tap **Export** (top right) → 1080p → save to phone.

## 8. Post it
Facebook first, then reshare to Instagram Reels + TikTok. Caption idea:
> Living my best life 🖤 Freedom looks good on me. → winwithdez.com
> *(no income promises; keep it real)*
