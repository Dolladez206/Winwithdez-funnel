## The giveaway (ready-to-publish copy)

### Title
**7 Questions That Expose a Fake "Work-From-Home" Offer in 60 Seconds**
*(#4 trips up almost everyone)*

### Intro
You've been burned before — or you've watched someone you love get burned. So
before you say yes to ANY "make money from home" offer, run it through these 7
questions. If it can't pass, walk away. (Yes — this one passes. That's why I'm
comfortable handing you the test.)

### The 7 questions
1. **Where does the money actually come from?** Real product sales to real
   customers — or mostly from signing new people up? (Real products = good sign.)
2. **What does it cost to start, and what do I get?** A low, clear cost with real
   value — or a big buy-in and vague promises?
3. **Do I have to recruit to win?** Can you benefit (e.g., save money) *without*
   ever selling to a soul? You should be able to.
4. **Are the income claims honest?** 🚩 If they promise you'll get rich, run.
   Honest offers say "results depend on your effort" — because they do.
5. **Is there pressure or a fake countdown?** Real opportunities are still there
   tomorrow. Pressure is a tactic, not a courtesy.
6. **Can I see the actual products, prices, and ingredients?** Transparency =
   trust. Hidden details = hidden problems.
7. **Can I walk away cheaply?** Low risk to exit means low risk to try.
